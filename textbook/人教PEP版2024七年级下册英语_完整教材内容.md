#### Grammar Focus 语法聚焦

**重点句型**：
- Can you swim? Yes, I can. / No, I can't.
- Can he play chess? Yes, he can. / No, he can't.
- Can Jane and Jill swim? Yes, they can. / No, they can't.
- What can you do? I can dance. / I can't sing.
- What club do you want to join? I want to join the chess club.

**语法讲解**：
情态动词can的用法：
1. 表示能力：I can swim. 我会游泳。
2. 表示请求：Can you help me? 你能帮我吗？
3. 表示许可：You can go now. 你现在可以走了。
4. 否定形式：can not = can't
5. 疑问句：Can + 主语 + 动词原形？

#### Grammar Focus 语法聚焦

**重点句型**：
- What time do you usually get up? I usually get up at six thirty.
- What time does Rick eat breakfast? He eats breakfast at seven o'clock.
- What time do they go to school? They go to school at eight o'clock.
- When does Jim play soccer? He plays soccer after school.
- When do your friends exercise? They usually exercise on weekends.

**语法讲解**：
What time/When引导的特殊疑问句：
1. What time 用于询问具体时间点
2. When 用于询问时间，可以指时间点或时间段
3. 结构：What time/When + do/does + 主语 + 动词原形？
4. 回答：主语 + 动词 + at + 时间点 / on + 星期/日期

#### Grammar Focus 语法聚焦

**重点句型**：
- How do you get to school? I ride my bike.
- How does he get to school? He takes the bus.
- How long does it take? It takes about 25 minutes.

**语法讲解**：
1. How 引导的特殊疑问句：
   - How do you get to school?（询问方式）
   - How long does it take?（询问时间长度）

2. 交通方式表达：
   - by + 交通工具（无冠词）：by bus, by bike
   - take + a/the + 交通工具：take a bus, take the subway
   - in/on + a/the + 交通工具：in a car, on a bus

  - Last Sunday, I went to...
  - We visited... / We saw...
  - I had a good time.

### 4. 如何写明信片
- **格式**：称呼、正文、结尾
- **常用表达**：
  - How's it going?
  - I'm having a great time in...
  - See you soon.

## 附录：常用表达汇总

### 问候与告别
- Good morning/afternoon/evening! 早上/下午/晚上好！
- Hello! / Hi! 你好！
- Goodbye! / Bye! 再见！
- See you later! 回头见！
- Have a good day! 祝你今天愉快！

### 感谢与道歉
- Thank you! / Thanks! 谢谢！
- You're welcome. 不客气。
- I'm sorry. 对不起。
- That's OK. / It's all right. 没关系。

### 请求与许可
- Can you help me? 你能帮我吗？
- Could you please...? 请你……好吗？
- May I...? 我可以……吗？
- Sure. / Of course. 当然可以。

### 询问信息
- What's this/that? 这/那是什么？
- Who is he/she? 他/她是谁？
- Where is it? 它在哪里？
- How much is it? 它多少钱？
- What time is it? 现在几点了？

### 谈论喜好
- What do you like? 你喜欢什么？
- I like... 我喜欢……
- I don't like... 我不喜欢……
- What's your favorite...? 你最喜欢的……是什么？
- My favorite... is... 我最喜欢的……是……
